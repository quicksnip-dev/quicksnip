The [QuickSnip Raycast extension](https://www.raycast.com/anders_morille/quicksnip) lets you search snippets and copy them to your clipboard without opening a browser.

It is built and maintained by the community ([@lille-morille](https://github.com/lille-morille)). Thank you!

## Install

1. Install [Raycast](https://www.raycast.com/) (macOS).
2. Open the [QuickSnip extension page](https://www.raycast.com/anders_morille/quicksnip) and click **Install Extension**.
3. Open Raycast and search for **QuickSnip**.

> The extension was built against the v1 API. If something looks out of date, open an issue on the extension's repository.
